# Knowledge — Meridian Bank

Everything in this file is sent to the chatbot with every message.
Replace the example bank below with the real one. Save the file — the server
reloads it by itself.

RULE: nothing private goes in this file. No customer names, no account numbers,
no internal procedures. Anything here can be shown to any visitor.

---

## About

Meridian Bank. Retail banking. 42 branches.
Customer service: 011 200 2000, open 24 hours.
Website: meridianbank.example

## Accounts we offer

**Everyday Account** — no monthly fee. Free debit card. No minimum balance.
**Savings Account** — 4.5% annual interest, paid monthly. Minimum 5,000 to open.
**Student Account** — no fee, ages 18 to 25, needs proof of enrolment.
**Business Account** — 1,200 per month. Includes 50 free transfers.

## Opening an account

Online at meridianbank.example/open, or at any branch.
You need: photo ID (passport or national ID), proof of address less than
3 months old, and your tax number.
Online applications are usually approved in 1 working day. Branch openings are
same day.

## Cards

Debit card arrives in 5 to 7 working days.
Replacement card: 500, free for the first one each year.
To activate: use the card at any Meridian ATM, or in the app under Cards.

## Daily limits

ATM withdrawal: 100,000 per day.
Online transfer to another bank: 500,000 per day.
Card payment: 300,000 per day.
Limits can be changed in the app under Settings, Limits. Increases take 24 hours.

## Transfers

Inside Meridian: instant, free.
To another local bank: same day if sent before 3:30 pm, otherwise next working day.
Fee 50 per transfer.
International: 3 to 5 working days, fee 2,500 plus the exchange margin.

## Fees

Monthly account fee: see "Accounts" above.
ATM withdrawal at a Meridian ATM: free.
ATM withdrawal at another bank: 150.
Failed direct debit: 750.
Paper statement by post: 300. Statements in the app are free.
Currency conversion on card payments abroad: 2.5%.

## Loans

**Personal loan** — 5,000 to 2,000,000. Terms 1 to 5 years. Rates from 14%,
depending on the application.
**Home loan** — up to 25 years. Rates from 11%. Needs a valuation and proof of income.
Applications are at a branch or in the app. A decision usually takes 3 working days.
We cannot tell anyone whether they will be approved before they apply.

## The app

Meridian Mobile, on the App Store and Google Play.
Reset a forgotten password in the app with "Forgot password", or call 011 200 2000.
If the app is locked after wrong passwords, it unlocks automatically after 30 minutes.

## Branches and hours

Monday to Friday: 9:00 am to 4:00 pm.
Saturday: 9:00 am to 1:00 pm, main branches only.
Sunday and public holidays: closed.
ATMs are open 24 hours.
Branch finder: meridianbank.example/branches

## Urgent — card lost, card stolen, or a payment the customer did not make

Call 011 200 2000 straight away. It is open 24 hours.
The card can also be frozen instantly in the app under Cards, Freeze.
Do not wait for a reply by chat or email.

## Complaints

Email complaints@meridianbank.example or fill the form at
meridianbank.example/complaints. We reply within 5 working days.

## Things this chatbot cannot do

- See any account, balance, or transaction
- Move money, cancel a payment, or change any limit
- Say whether a loan or card application will be approved
- Give advice on which product a person should choose
